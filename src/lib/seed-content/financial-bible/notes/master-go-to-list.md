# "Master Go-To List" (Line of Credit List doc from 6FF Mastery L24) — paraphrased

## Business LOC — no / low doc
- KeyBank: open the business account, leave it ~1 week, apply for the BLOC first, then go back for the cards.
- Liberty Bank (CT, RI, MA): no doc, can apply by phone; lines ≈ 25% of gross revenue; hard pull TransUnion + FICO SBSS; 680+.
- Bank of the Sierra (CA): ≤$25k no doc, above that docs; open a business account first, then apply online.
- Owners Bank (CT, FL, GA, MA, NC, PA, RI, SC, TX): same data points as Liberty; online app asks for 3 months bank statements.
- Woodforest National Bank:
  - Business Reli unsecured LOC $500–$10k, Experian hard pull (Numerated platform), doesn't report to business bureaus, 1 yr business tax return, fixed ~17%.
  - Business revolving LOC: $5k min, secured by business assets up to 80% LTV (not real estate), 2 yrs business + personal returns, YTD P&L, debt schedule, Equifax hard pull, Prime + 3%.
  - Executive LOC: $25k–$150k, in branch, reports personally, Equifax, Prime + 2–2.5%, $125k+ AGI, 2 yrs personal returns, $500k net worth or $100k investable assets.
- Fundation (renaming to Quantum Fintech): soft pull Experian personal + business; UCC on business assets; may only approve businesses with established business credit; 3 months bank statements; doesn't lend in HI, NV, MA, MI, NJ, NY, ND, OH, PA, PR, TN; can approve several products.
- Under $50k, "no UCC / no 4506" notes: BMO (4506 yes) · PNC · Fifth Third · Wells Fargo (BLOC up to $99k, no UCC) · Commerce Bank (MO, KS, IL, OK, CO; 4506 yes) · M&T (no UCC, no 4506) · Truist (no UCC, 4506 yes) · SECU (no UCC, no 4506) · Security Plus (no 4506 under $100k, no UCC) · Tompkins (no 4506, no UCC under $50k) · Ameris (up to $100k on a P&L; UCC normally; checks website, LexisNexis business report, Equifax personal).

## Online business card apps
- Truist business card · GM business card · Amex: up to 4 business cards + business LOC (LOC wants ~$3k/mo and a healthy linked balance via Plaid) · BMO: card if business <2 yrs, BLOC if 2+ yrs (TransUnion) · UMB: no doc to $25k (AZ, CO, IL, KS, MS, NE, OK, TX, UT).

## Fintech / bank-statement-only (600+ score and $25k+/month deposits)
- Capital on Tap (links business bank account) · BILL Divvy · Bluevine (640+, $40k/mo revenue) · FlexOne (680+, net-60) · Toolbox · Loot (650+, $250k revenue) · Amex Business Line of Credit (660+, hard pull) · Fundbox · Finfare · Ascendus LOC (575+, 1 yr in business) · NorthOne (620+, $2k balance).

## No-doc personal loans (bureau pulled)
- Navy Federal — TransUnion · PenFed — Equifax · USAA — Equifax.

## Alternative lenders that pay referral commissions (no UCC)
- Jumpstart (Unsecured Funding Source): buying a business or franchise; doesn't look at business financials; doesn't report personally; 750+ on all bureaus; $50k+ personal liquidity; up to $450k; registered business; current W-2/1099 income. Pays 1% (1–4 deals) / 2% (5+).
- Toolbox: BLOC up to $250k at 16–18%, $250k revenue + 6 months in business, no draw/origination/prepay fees; corporate charge card up to $1M/month; Plaid + ID & face scan. Pays $250 per funded referral.
- Lendontics (BCC USA): LOW DOC 700+, ID, 3 months statements, ~5-day turnaround, up to $250k. FULL DOC 660+, debt schedule, PFS, 6 months statements, AR/AP aging, 3 yrs business + personal returns. Commission 3% to $150k, 2.75% to $300k, 2.5% to $450k, 2.25% to $600k, 2% to $750k.
- Backd BLOC: $100k/month businesses, 2 yrs, tax returns, 650+ Experian, 20% initial draw required; you earn 3% of every draw (e.g. $50k draw → $1,500). Partner portal has close-rate requirements — don't blast the link publicly.
- Loot: BLOC $5k–$250k, soft pull, $250k revenue, 650+ Experian; MCA from 500+ score with $25k revenue.

## Removed from this list on purpose
- "Shelf corp or foreign entity gets the client into the footprint" and "use the same bank account across multiple entities" — a foreign registration is fine only if the business really operates there; shelf corps and recycled accounts misrepresent the borrower.
