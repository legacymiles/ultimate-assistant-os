# Banker call script — questions to ask every bank (from the "Master Bank Questions" doc, 6FF L116; paraphrased)

Opener (use your own true details): who you are, that you run a business-funding/consulting firm, that you have clients in their area, and you want their requirements before sending anyone in.

Ask:
1. Requirements for business credit cards? Business lines of credit? Business loans?
2. Which bureau do you pull for business cards? Do you also check Experian Business / business credit?
3. Same bureau for LOCs and loans?
4. Maximum total business card exposure per person? Exposure limits personal vs business?
5. Can one person apply for two LLCs on the same day?
6. Two quick scenarios — what would each qualify for? (e.g. 2-yr business, ~$100k/yr, 720 score; 4-yr plumbing business, $1M+, 700 score)
7. Do you take virtual/online applications from outside the branch area?
8. If a business registers as a foreign entity in your state, can it access your products? Which states can you serve?
9. How much can a line/loan be before you file a UCC?
10. Max line/loan before you require financials (tax returns / bank statements)?
11. Do you lend off gross or net revenue?
12. Do you run a 4506-C (IRS transcript)?
13. Underwriting in-house or outsourced? Is there a reconsideration path?
14. Industries you won't fund?
15. Equipment loans — how do they work?
16. Any referral/affiliate program for accounts or loans you send?
17. What's the process to start sending applications?
