Jarvis Record

Saves what you type into job and benefits applications, and fills the next one from what it already knows.

Connection

Mint the token in Jarvis → Record → Connect your browser. It is shown once.

Numbers that stay on this computer

These are never sent to your hub and never reach an AI — Jarvis strips them before anything is stored. They live here, in this browser, so the extension can still type them into a form. Leave them empty and it will simply skip those fields.

What this extension can reach. Nothing, until you click its button on a page. There is no background script watching your browsing and no list of sites it runs on — each read is granted for one tab, by one click, and expires when you navigate away. It reads form fields and it never touches a password, a payment field or a CAPTCHA.
